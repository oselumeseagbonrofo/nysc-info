STEP 02 — IN DETAIL

NERD clearance, demystified.

NERD = National Education Records Database. It verifies your final-year project / thesis is genuine. Since Jan/Feb 2026, NYSC requires the NERD code at registration.

COST
₦12k – ₦15k
Negotiable. Price it down. Avoid touts — use accredited centres only.
YOU NEED
Signed project + certificate or statement of result
Signatures: you + supervisor + HOD.
YOU GET
NERD code / clearance letter
Issued after upload. Keep it.

How to do it

  1. Get the signed copy. Print your final project. Collect signatures (you, supervisor, HOD). Ask your department for the signing window.
  2. Find an accredited centre. Use the official lookup — pick the closest centre and call ahead. Lookup NERD centres →
  3. Upload & pay. Centre uploads the signed project, you pay (₦12k–₦15k). You receive the NERD clearance code/letter.
  4. Use it at NYSC registration. Enter/upload the NERD code on the NYSC portal (Sept 7–13). Without it, you cannot submit.
💡

Tip from experience: call at least two centres for quotes, go early in the day, and ask for the receipt with your NERD code clearly printed. The portal gets busy — do NERD at least a week before Sept 7.

No certificate yet? Use Statement of Result.

Many schools have not issued certificates by Batch C. NYSC accepts statement of result in place of certificate for both NERD and registration.

Copied ✓

Send to: Registry + Student Affairs, CC: Sylvester Efomah. Some schools send a PDF (unlikely) — otherwise collect physically. Budget ₦10k per transcript if you also need transcripts for masters/PAN applications (3 schools = ~₦30k).

Foreign-trained graduates

Additional step: credentials evaluated by the Federal Ministry of Education, plus pre-camp physical verification (usually 2–3 days before ICT processing). Bring degree, transcript, passport.

After registration

ICT processes your data, then your call-up number appears on the dashboard. The full call-up letter (state of deployment + camp address) is printable a few days before camp. Not everyone gets it at the same time.

Lagos posting note: Call-up numbers are used for deployment requests. If you are aiming to serve in Lagos, have your number ready once it is issued and follow the official posting rules — beware anyone promising posting for a fee.
