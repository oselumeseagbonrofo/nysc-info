STAGE 1 Profile creation (at home)
0
CHECK FIRSTIs it open?

Look for the active registration card

Open portal.nysc.org.ng/NYSC/ or portal.nysc.org.ng/nysc1/ — both are official NYSC addresses; use whichever loads for you. Under Open Registration Windows, No Active Registration means the portal is not open yet. Keep checking back. The day it opens, that section is replaced with the batch and its From / To dates. The red arrow in the second screenshot marks the button: click New Registration to start your profile.

The operator's order: everybody does this profile-creation stage before going to any accredited centre in the morning to continue the registration.
1
ACCOUNTInitiate account creation

Email in twice, and mind the warnings

The Initiate Account Creation page opens with two red warnings. One: never create a second account. If you registered in a previous batch or started once before, do not create again; a double account will impede your registration. Two: Yahoo domains (yahoo.com, yahoo.co.uk, ymail.com, rocketmail.com) suffer delayed mail delivery, so the portal tells you to use another email provider (Gmail is a safe pick).

Type your email address, then type the same email again in the confirm box.

2
SECRET QUESTIONYour lifeline

Set your lifeline, then watch your inbox

Pick your secret question. That is what retrieves your password if you ever forget it, so write it down somewhere real. Submit, then go straight to your email and check whether the activation mail has dropped.

Give it 10 to 20 minutes. Nothing? Repeat the whole process with another email. Both emails fail to drop? Do not panic, it is a known NYSC issue and centres see it every batch. Keep trying, and check spam and promotions while you wait.

3
ACTIVATIONPre payment page

Fill in NIN, institution, JAMB

Click the activation link in the mail. It opens the Pre Payment Page. Fill it in: your email address, your NIN number, your institution (university or polytechnic), your JAMB registration number and matric number where asked, and country of study (Nigeria). Open Check Senate List or its equivalents while you are there and confirm your name is on it. Tick Refresh NIMC if it asks to re-pull your NIN details.

With the details correct, the page fetches the rest from your institution automatically, your grade included. Then choose the password you want for NYSC, type it twice, submit.

4
DONEStage one complete

Profile creation is over. Biometrics is next.

That closes the profile creation stage. Stage two is biometrics, and you cannot do it yourself. Locate any NYSC accredited centre close to you for it.

STAGE 2 Biometrics (at an accredited centre)
Fingerprints and a webcam photo, taken at the centre. If biometrics keep failing, stay patient while the operator retries; it eventually passes. Wear solid colours (black works), keep ears visible and eyes open. Ladies, pack your hair so face and ears show clearly.
STAGE 3 Bio-data (centre, or careful self-service)
✦
BIO-DATAThe full form

What the form asks for

The centre operator calls this stage straightforward. If you know what you are doing you can do it yourself; to avoid errors, leave it for the centre to process.

The form covers: personal identification (matric number, JAMB reg number, names, date and place of birth, gender, marital status, passport photo), health status, state and LGA of origin, present and permanent address, two next of kin plus a contact in case of death, education from primary to tertiary with dates and awards, military / paramilitary / national award status, Nigerian languages you speak, states you have visited, and your shoe and shirt sizes.

The graduation-date trap: a wrong date of graduation can deny you camping. Write the exact date as it appears on your Senate list. After submission, logout and login again for the NYSC payment. The cafe man should help with this.
TWELVE FROM THE CENTREAdvice for PCMs

Straight from the operator

01Check spam and promotions. The confirmation link lands there sometimes.
02The portal will slow down. Thousands register at once. Be patient.
03If the site hangs, do not close the browser. Keep refreshing until it responds.
04Webcam verification: solid colours, black works. Ears visible, eyes open.
05Ladies: pack your hair so face and ears show. Dress decently.
06Biometrics failing? Be patient while the operator keeps trying. Do not give up.
07NIN name must match the Senate list name. Top cause of "details not found".
08Do not go to the cafe if your name is not on the Senate list yet.
09Errors happen because the NYSC system is not perfect. Not always your fault.
10Keep trying. The system eventually lets you through the steps.
11Plan for delays. Registration can take longer than you expect.
12Pick a confident operator who explains the process clearly. Avoid unsure ones.
