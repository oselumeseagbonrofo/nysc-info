Is your name on the list?

Your school uploads graduates to the NYSC portal. If you are not there, nothing after this works. Check with matriculation number, surname and date of birth.

Check Senate List Not found → Student Affairs / Mobilization officer, now.
2
DO THIS NOWBefore 7 Sept

Get NERD clearance

Upload the signed final-year project at an accredited centre. You get a code / letter. You will paste it into the NYSC portal.

  • Signatures: you + supervisor + HOD
  • Only accredited centres — lookup here
  • Keep the receipt with the code
3
7 DAYS ONLY

Register on the portal

portal.nysc.org.ng — 7 to 13 September. Use a personal email and phone you control. Upload NERD when asked. Print Green Card in colour. Do not laminate.

Fee for online call-up printing: ₦3,000

4

Collect your call-up

After ICT processing, your call-up number appears first, then the letter (state, camp address, reporting date). Printed in phases — not everyone at once. Print in colour, three copies.

Foreign-trained: complete pre-camp physical verification before this stage.

5

Go to camp

21 days. Registration cut-off is 2–3 days after gates open. Arrive early. If you miss it, you roll to Stream 2.

Carry originals + photocopies. Medical fitness cert (government hospital) is mandatory at the gate.
